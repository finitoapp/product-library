# Picture style

Every picture in the library is one set: a merchant's item grid shows dozens
of them side by side, so they must share angle, light, background and scale.
A picture's prompt is the style below with the product's `image.subject`
put in place of `{subject}`. Change the style only for the whole set — then
regenerate every picture.

```text
Photorealistic product photograph for a café and restaurant point-of-sale menu tile.
Square 1:1 composition. Subject: {subject}.
The subject is centered and fills about 70 % of the frame, seen from a 30-degree elevated front angle.
Soft natural daylight from the upper left, a gentle soft shadow to the lower right.
Plain seamless warm light-grey background (#ECE9E4), nothing else in the frame except the serving itself (cup, glass, plate, saucer).
Plain unbranded white or clear tableware.
No text, no labels, no logos, no brand names, no hands, no people, no cutlery unless part of the serving.
Appetizing, clean, realistic portions, typical of a Central European café, bistro or pub.
```

## Writing a subject

- Describe what the guest is served, the way it is served: vessel, portion,
  the visible detail that tells it apart from its neighbours (a cappuccino's
  foam cap versus a latte macchiato's layers in a tall glass).
- One serving, no brand: "a pint glass of golden lager with a white foam
  head", never a named beer.
- Keep it to one sentence; the style already says light, background and
  framing.
